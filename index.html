Features

Everything you need to stay consistent. Nothing you don't.

Streak tracking

Current and best streaks for every habit, with a grace day so one late check-in never wipes your progress.

Weekly targets

Not every habit is daily. Set a target of 3, 5 or 7 days a week and track progress toward it.

Charts & heatmap

Daily completion bars, streak comparisons and a heatmap that shows your consistency at a glance.

Categories & search

Group habits into Health, Deep Work, Mindfulness and Growth, then filter or search in an instant.

Satisfying feedback

Subtle check-off sounds and celebrations when you hit a goal. Mute them any time.

Private by design

Your data lives in your browser. No account, no tracking, no server storing your routines.
